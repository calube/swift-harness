# Feature: Save a recipient and postcode

The app builds, launches, and its first screen shows how many posts it loaded. Keep the existing
tests passing.

## What to build

A shipping address form on the first screen, under the posts count. Nothing here talks to the
network.

- **Recipient**: required. Trim spaces at both ends, then it must be 2 to 50 characters.
- **Postcode**: required. Exactly 5 digits, such as `90210`.
- Show each field's error under the field as soon as its value is invalid, and only then.
- **Save** is disabled while any field is invalid. Saving stores the address on the device, so the
  next launch opens with the saved values.
- **Clear** empties every field and clears every error, without touching the saved address.

Keep the storage behind a dependency so tests don't touch the disk.

## Acceptance tests

1. A recipient of fewer than 2 characters after trimming shows "Recipient must be 2 to 50 characters" and Save stays disabled.
2. A postcode that is not exactly 5 digits shows "Enter a 5-digit postcode" and Save stays
   disabled.
3. Saving a valid address stores it, and the next launch shows the saved values.
4. Clear empties every field and clears every error, and the saved address is unchanged.

## Out of scope

More than one address, address lookup, and syncing to a server.
